Read `CLAUDE.md` at the repo root and follow it. It is the source of truth
for architecture, commands, conventions, and quality gates, including the
app-specific rules in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`, `apps/api/CLAUDE.md`, and

`.claude/agents/`), use the OpenCode equivalent (`question` tool,
